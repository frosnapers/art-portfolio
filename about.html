Lisa Apers About

Lisa Apers is a Belgian visual artist based in Denmark, working across sculpture, installation and various media.

Her practice looks at the ways we organise everyday life in the objects we surround ourselves with, the habits we adopt and the norms we rarely question.

Working with familiar forms and materials, she shifts their function, context or meaning to create moments of friction, humour and ambiguity. The work often sits somewhere between the ordinary and the slightly strange, inviting a closer look at what we consider normal.
