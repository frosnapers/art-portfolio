Concrete and glass

What makes art, art? Does it reside in the curation, or is there no art at all?

Concrete Wine Glasses merges the artworks with the peripheral elements and activities of the exhibition space. As the focus shifts from art to wine drinking, the objects fade into each other.

A concrete experience of discomfort, set against the elegance of the gallery opening.

As we move through our nine-to-fives, we often forget that a regular work day only takes up eight hours of the day. The rest is ours to shape. The 925 clock challenges the constant pursuit of efficiency in a productivity centred society, inviting us to reconsider how we perceive and allocate our days. Rather than letting our lives revolve around our jobs and “staying productive", it recognises the importance of time necessary to spend for one to simply be, and allows the viewer to do so in an alternate timezone.
